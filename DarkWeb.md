# Dark Web

## What is the Dark Web?
The dark web is a hidden part of the internet that is not indexed by traditional search engines like Google or Bing. It requires special software, such as Tor (The Onion Router) or I2P (Invisible Internet Project), to access

## Understanding the Dark Web

The dark web exists within the deep web, which includes all the web content that is not publicly accessible, such as:
- Private email accounts
- Banking portals
- Corporate databases
The dark web, however, is intentionally hidden and often used for anonymity.

## How to Stay Safe on the Dark Web
- **Use a VPN** – Ensures online anonymity.
- **Update Security Software** – Keeps your device protected from threats.
- **Avoid Suspicious Links** – Prevents malware and scams.
- **Do Not Share Personal Information** – Protects against identity theft.
- **Use Strong Passwords** – Enhances account security.
- **Secure Cryptocurrency Transactions** – Reduces the risk of theft.
